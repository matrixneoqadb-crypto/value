## Document summary

The PDF records an **indicative replacement/development-cost assessment** for MATRIX Star dated **21 August 2026**.

- **Professional recreation of the currently specified scope:** **£20 million–£60 million+**.
- **Fully validated and deployed multi-domain programme:** potentially **£100 million+**.
- These figures represent replacement or development expenditure—not a company valuation, transaction price, or audited appraisal.[1]

## Scope assessed

The estimate treats MATRIX Star as a broad technology programme comprising:

- MATRIX/MXAI core architecture.
- Multi-agent orchestration through MPAI, MXAI, EAI and EAI2.
- Mathematical, verification, provenance and evidence-control systems.
- Security and human-approval gates.
- Application modules and engineering concepts.
- Composite 6336, 6-3-3-6, 4444, 4-4-4-4 and 16-node architectures.
- Multidisciplinary engineering, AI/ML, security, QA, infrastructure, documentation and programme-management effort.[1]

## CERN boundary

The document explicitly does **not** claim that MATRIX Star has CERN-equivalent infrastructure, implementation, experimental proof or physical quantum capability. It limits the comparison to systems architecture and states that CERN retains the demonstrated advantage in physical quantum execution, experimental hardware, calibration, scientific publication and independent evidence.[1]

**Bottom line:** the document supports a **£20m–£60m+ indicative professional recreation cost**, with **£100m+** possible for a mature, independently validated and deployed programme. It does not establish a market valuation or prove physical quantum execution.
